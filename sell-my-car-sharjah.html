How Ownership Transfer Actually Works in Sharjah

Sharjah has no direct transfer — here is the real sequence, step by step.

Note: This is different from Dubai and Abu Dhabi, where ownership can transfer in one visit. In Sharjah, the plates must be cancelled and the car re-registered under the new owner as separate steps.

Export Inspection

The car must first pass an export inspection before anything else can happen.

Plate Cancellation

The plates are cancelled ("shatb") through the MOI or TAM app.

Hand In the Plates

The cancelled plates are handed over at an approved licensing centre in Sharjah.

Possession Transfers to the Buyer

Only after the plates are cancelled does possession of the car move to the buyer.

Registration Inspection & Insurance

The car goes through a registration inspection, and insurance is checked.

Register & Pay Fees via the MOI App

The buyer registers the car through the MOI app and pays the applicable fees.

Collect New Plates & Ownership

New plates and the new ownership certificate are issued at an approved licensing centre.

Sharjah FAQ

Is ownership transfer in Sharjah the same as Dubai or Abu Dhabi?

No. Sharjah has no direct ownership transfer. The car must first pass an export inspection, then the plates are cancelled via the MOI or TAM app and handed in at an approved licensing centre. Only then does possession move to the buyer, followed by a registration inspection, insurance check, registration via the MOI app, fee payment, and collecting new plates and ownership from an approved licensing centre.

Do I have to handle all this paperwork myself?

No — we guide you through every step of the export inspection, plate cancellation and registration process, so you don't have to figure it out on your own.

How fast can I sell my car in Sharjah?

The cash offer and payment happen the same day. The government process itself takes a bit longer than Dubai or Abu Dhabi because of the export inspection and plate cancellation steps.

Do you buy financed cars registered in Sharjah?

Yes — we deal directly with your bank to settle the outstanding loan and deduct it from our offer.

Do you buy cars with unpaid traffic fines?

Yes, we buy cars even with outstanding traffic fines — we take care of settling them as part of the sale.
