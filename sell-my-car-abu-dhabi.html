What Selling a Car in Abu Dhabi With Us Actually Looks Like

We buy any car — any make, any model, any condition — and most of our Abu Dhabi deals move just as quickly as Dubai: from your first message to cash in hand, many customers finish the whole process the same day.

Ownership transfer in Abu Dhabi can be done through the TAM app or the Ministry of Interior's website, moi.gov.ae — or in person at a vehicle licensing centre if you prefer, so there's no need to wait for a specific office to be free.

The transfer itself is quick: once both sides are ready and the paperwork is in order, it typically takes under 30 minutes, just like in Dubai.

As a trusted used car buyer in Abu Dhabi, we deal with you directly — no dealer commissions, no middlemen. Whether it's a sedan, SUV, or commercial vehicle, we give Abu Dhabi sellers a fair, fast cash offer for used cars in any condition, running or not.

Two things to have ready before the transfer: you'll need valid insurance on the car (either transferred to the new owner or a new policy) before ownership can move — and if the car's registration has expired, it needs to pass an inspection first.

This exact process — TAM app or moi.gov.ae, insurance requirement, expired-registration inspection — applies the same way in Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, and Umm Al Quwain, so wherever you are in the Northern Emirates, we handle it the same way.
